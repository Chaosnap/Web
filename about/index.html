Hi, I'm GuanHua Yu, though Yuki works too.

I make websites, UI/UX, videos, music, and the occasional thing I can't really explain why I suddenly decided to build. This site is basically what happens when all those interests pile up in one place.

I'm not very interested in packaging myself into a perfectly coherent persona, so this site isn't trying to be especially formal either. Think of it as my room on the internet, a filing cabinet, or a slightly over-designed personal junk drawer.

I don't talk a lot and I'm not very good at performing enthusiasm. If I seem a little cold or too direct sometimes, it's probably not personal — that's just my default setting.

Anyway, have a look around. If you find something you like, that's already pretty nice.


你好，我是 GuanHua Yu，也可以叫我 Yuki。

我会做网页、UI/UX、视频、音乐，以及一些很难解释自己为什么突然想做的东西。这里基本就是这些兴趣最后堆在一起之后产生的结果。

我不太喜欢把自己包装成一个很完整的人设，所以这个网站也没有打算特别正式。你可以把它当成我的网络房间、文件柜，或者一个稍微设计过头的个人垃圾堆。

我话不算很多，也不太会热情营业。偶尔看起来有点冷、有点直接，大概率不是针对谁，只是我的默认状态。

总之，随便看看。要是刚好有喜欢的东西，那就很好。
