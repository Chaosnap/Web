I'm GuanHua Yu. Around this site I also use Yuki. This is less a formal portfolio than a personal room on the web — somewhere to keep notes, music, design experiments and small things I do not want to lose.

I like interfaces that feel touched by a person. That is why this site keeps oversized borders, fake windows, folders, rulers and an unreasonable number of tiny buttons.

I'm quieter than the sites I build. I would rather let the work, the notes and the odd little details do most of the talking.

Feel free to reach out about something here, or just say hi. Bilibili, Discord and email are all linked on the contact page.


你好，我是 GuanHua Yu，在这个网站里也会使用 Yuki 这个称呼。这里不是特别正式的作品集，更像是我在互联网上留的一间房间：放笔记、音乐、网页实验，以及一些舍不得让它们消失的小东西。

我喜欢“看得出有人动过手”的界面，所以故意保留了厚边框、假窗口、文件夹、标尺和很多其实没必要存在的小按钮。它们可能不够高效，但更像我自己的地方。

我本人比这个网站安静一点。比起把自己包装成一个非常完整的人设，我更愿意把注意力留给内容本身。想了解我，可以从档案、笔记和这些小细节慢慢看。
